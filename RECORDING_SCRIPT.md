## Pre-Flight Recording Checklist

1. **Local Server Verification:** Ensure `http://127.0.0.1:8000` loads properly with the black radar favicon in the browser tab and `406 jobs indexed` in the header pill.
2. **Browser Window:** Press `F11` (or maximize window) at 100% zoom.
3. **Audio:** Speak with natural pacing and steady volume. No corporate buzzwords or rushed delivery.
4. **Post-Recording:** Save the MP4, upload to YouTube as **Unlisted** (or copy the Loom link), and keep the link ready for submission.
