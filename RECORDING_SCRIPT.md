## Pre-Flight Recording Checklist

1. **Local Server Verification:** Ensure `http://127.0.0.1:8000` loads properly with the black radar favicon in the browser tab and `415 jobs indexed` in the header pill.
2. **Browser Window:** Press `F11` (or maximize window) at 100% zoom.
3. **Cache Status:** `search_cache` is cleared. Clicking **Fetch from Google Jobs** will trigger a live SerpApi query and display the green `[LIVE]` status badge on camera.
4. **Audio:** Speak with natural pacing and steady volume. No corporate buzzwords or rushed delivery.
5. **Post-Recording:** Save the MP4, upload to YouTube as **Unlisted** (or copy the Loom link), and keep the link ready for submission.
